# Scope and Coverage Boundaries

**Status:** provisional. Do not infer a final chapter outline.

- **Full internal treatment:** theory and engineering methods essential to correct coherent receiver design decisions.
- **Selective internal treatment:** supporting disciplines developed to the depth needed to apply their results correctly.
- **External/assumed:** broad standard RF foundations and device-specific/current facts; provide precise references and bridge essential coherent-receiver reasoning internally.

Assess each subject by (1) conceptual understanding, (2) quantitative analytical competence, (3) design and implementation decisions, (4) verification and failure recognition. Extensive coverage in one dimension does not imply extensive coverage in all dimensions.

No fixed chapter/page count. Re-evaluate boundaries using contrasting receiver designs. Unknowns should remain explicitly unresolved.
