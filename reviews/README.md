# Technical Review Records

Review records must identify the exact source revision and claims assessed; never record a human approval that has not happened. See `templates/technical-review.md` and `standards/verification-and-acceptance.md`.

**Status distinctions:** owner-scoped provisional acceptances are recorded in the
project decisions, including ADR-0005 and ADR-0006. They do not establish qualified
human specialist validation. Separate-context AI findings are labeled AI-assisted;
only actual engineer examination establishes the standard’s human Reviewed state.
