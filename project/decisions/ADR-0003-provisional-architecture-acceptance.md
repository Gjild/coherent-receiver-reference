## Qualifications and limitations

1. Learning prerequisites and application treatment requirements must allow motivating examples before full quantitative methods are taught.
2. K02–K07 mathematical bridges must be developed for the declared experienced-RF-engineer baseline without hidden specialist prerequisites.
3. Interferometry, vector antenna/polarization models and application-processing scope require further boundary review; essential coherent-receiver reasoning must remain internal.
4. Educational and engineering adequacy must be tested through actual textbook development, including the later dual-channel relative-phase-stability pilot.

Acceptance concerns suitability for planning only. It does not independently validate any mathematical derivation, physical model, engineering method, simulation, measurement or hardware-performance claim, and does not establish system compliance. Existing uncertainties, claim-level source work and specialist review obligations remain open. External AI review assistance is not independent physical evidence; no unprovided review report or findings are claimed here.

## Consequences and stopping boundary

Capability definitions, canonical treatment specifications and reference-design briefs are preserved. Boundary questions carry into planning under these qualifications; acceptance does not resolve technical adequacy or authorize silent scope changes. The architecture remains provisional and revisable on development evidence and further owner decisions.

[Current work](../current-work.md) identifies provisional book architecture and annotated table-of-contents development as the next stage, **not yet started**. This closeout does not execute it. No table of contents, chapter, derivation, engineering model, reference-design execution or publishing infrastructure is developed. Closeout checks and their limits are recorded in current work.
